    VetCare — Animal Health Management Platform

    Project Overview

    VetCare is a cross-platform digital veterinary healthcare management system developed as my final-year university project. It consists of Android and web-based applications designed to improve animal healthcare management by connecting pet owners and veterinary professionals through a centralized digital platform.

    Problem

    Traditional animal healthcare management often involves fragmented medical records, limited access to veterinary services, and inefficient appointment coordination. VetCare addresses these challenges by providing a centralized platform for managing pet information, monitoring animal health, maintaining medical records, and facilitating communication between pet owners and veterinarians.

    Solution

    Designed and developed an Android and web-based veterinary management platform using Flutter and Firebase, incorporating user authentication, pet profiles, digital health records, appointment management, veterinary service discovery, and animal health monitoring. The system was designed to support communication between pet owners and veterinary professionals while providing a foundation for intelligent symptom assessment and digital veterinary care.

    Key Features

    • Role-based authentication for pet owners and veterinary professionals.
    • Pet profile creation and animal health information management.
    • Digital veterinary records and health history tracking.
    • Appointment scheduling and veterinary service management.
    • Veterinarian directory for discovering veterinary professionals.
    • Animal symptom monitoring and proposed intelligent health assessment.
    • Communication and telemedicine support for remote veterinary consultations.

    Technology Stack

    Flutter · Dart · Firebase Authentication · Cloud Firestore · Firebase Storage · Cloud Functions

    Academic Contribution

    As my final-year Computer Science project, VetCare provided practical experience in cross-platform application development, UI/UX design, cloud-based database integration, authentication, software architecture, and solving real-world problems through technology. I contributed to the system's design, user interfaces, authentication workflows, and core application functionality.
